Stage 2 — Spec it with AI

Turn the idea into a tight build brief

Don't start building. Make a chat AI define the smallest version for you.

Inputs · what you told the AI

  • The one action on first open?
    Type a few ingredients and hit "Find recipes".
  • Inputs → outputs?
    In: a comma list of ingredients. Out: a grid of recipe cards, ranked by how many of your ingredients they use.
  • The magic moment?
    Seeing a real recipe you could actually start now, made from what you typed.
  • What data can we fake?
    A handful of believable recipes with titles, cook times, and steps.
  • What to leave OUT?
    Accounts, saving favourites, grocery delivery, nutrition, social feed.

Outputs · what the AI gave back

One-screen spec

  • One user: the broke, busy student with a half-empty fridge.
  • Single core action: ingredients in → ranked recipes out.
  • Inputs → outputs: comma-separated ingredients → grid of recipe cards → tap for full recipe.
  • Fake for the demo: 5–6 recipes with title, time, ingredients, steps.
  • Out of scope: accounts, saving, delivery, nutrition, social.
▷ The coach prompt (paste into any chat AI)
Act as a product coach. I want to build FridgeChef. Define: the one user, the single core action, the inputs and outputs of that one screen, what data to fake for a demo, and what to explicitly leave OUT. Keep it to one screen buildable tonight.
