2 · Three demand signals

  • ✅ Search demand — FOUND
    "YNAB alternatives 2026" and "Mint alternatives 2026" are high-volume terms; Mint shut down in 2024, leaving millions hunting for a replacement. Sources: NerdWallet, Envelope Budgeting.
  • ✅ People complaining — FOUND
    Top reasons users leave YNAB: $109/yr price, steep learning curve, sync issues. No app is praised for answering "can we afford this?" in plain English. Source: NerdWallet review roundup.
  • ✅ Someone already making money — FOUND
    YNAB ($109/yr) and Monarch Money (~$99/yr, fastest-growing post-Mint) prove willingness to pay. The free + AI + couples slice is open. Source: Pine AI.

Verdict: all three signals green.

3 · Back-of-napkin market sizing

  • TAM
    ~2.7M Canadian couples in the "merging money" phase × $20/yr affiliate value ≈ $54M CAD/yr.
  • SAM
    ~400k tech-comfortable couples actively seeking a Mint/YNAB alternative × $20/yr ≈ $8M CAD/yr.
  • SOM (next 12 mo)
    1,000–5,000 active couples via Demo Night + referral loop ≈ $20k–$100k CAD.

Anchor: global budget-apps market ~$247M–$4.8B USD (2025, estimates vary). Source: Business Research Insights.

4 · The wedge

Why you: the "where did it all go?" conversation is one most couples have had — that emotional insight drives every design call toward feeling in control, not data completeness.

Why now: Mint's 2024 shutdown freed millions of users, AI chat is now the expected UX, and cost-of-living pressure makes shared control urgent.

One-liner: "The budgeting app for new couples that answers 'can we afford this?' instead of just showing you charts."
