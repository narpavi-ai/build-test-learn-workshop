Stage 2 — Spec it with AI

Kora — one-screen spec

Ask a money question. Get a straight answer and one confident number.

Inputs · what you told the AI

  • First-time core action
    Ask the AI a plain-English money question — e.g. "Can we afford dinner out this weekend?"
  • Magic moment
    The "safe to spend" number — one big, confident figure that appears without any spreadsheet math.
  • Out of scope for the demo
    Accounts / login; real bank connection; two-user live sync.

Outputs · what the AI gave back

The one user

A newly-married couple in their first year of combined finances — two incomes, two old habits, no shared system yet. Tech-comfortable, not spreadsheet-lovers.

Single core action

Open the app → type a money question in plain English → get an instant AI answer + a "safe to spend this week" number.

Inputs → Outputs

  • In
    A plain-English question in a chat-style box — e.g. "Can we afford dinner out this weekend?" or "How are we tracking on groceries?"
  • Out
    A direct AI answer (2–3 sentences) grounded in the spending data, plus one big highlighted number: Safe to spend this week: $147.

Data to fake for the demo

~20 hand-written transactions for one realistic couple-month: rent, groceries (×4), streaming subscriptions (×3), dining out (×3), gas, utilities, gym, coffee runs. Income ≈ $6,200. Never "lorem ipsum" — names and amounts should feel lived-in.

Explicitly out of scope

  • Accounts, login, or profiles — demo opens straight to the screen
  • Real bank connection (Plaid / open banking) — fake data only
  • Two-user login and live sync — show one shared view
  • Saved history, favourites, multi-month views

Coach prompt — paste into any chat AI to regenerate this

▷ Paste into ChatGPT / Claude
Act as a product coach for a non-technical student founder. I want to build an AI budgeting app for a newly-married couple merging finances for the first time. Define: (1) the one user, (2) the single core action on first open, (3) the inputs and outputs of that one screen, (4) what data to fake for a believable demo, and (5) what to explicitly leave OUT. Keep it to one screen buildable in an evening.
