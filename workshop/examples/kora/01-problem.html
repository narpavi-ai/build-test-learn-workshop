Outputs · what the AI gave back

Problem statement

Newly-married couples merging their money for the first time struggle to keep household spending under control because neither partner has a single, up-to-date picture of where the money actually goes. Today they keep a spreadsheet they barely update, which goes stale within weeks and costs them money in forgotten subscriptions, overlapping bills, and budget-blowing months they only notice once it's too late.

Supporting points

  • Who they are
    A couple in their first year of combined finances — two incomes, two sets of old habits, no shared system yet. Tech-comfortable but not spreadsheet-lovers.
  • The moment the pain hits
    End of the month, or when a card statement / surprise bill lands and neither of them saw it coming — then the "where did it all go?" conversation.
  • What "solved" feels like
    Both partners open one app and instantly see what's safe to spend this week, plus an early heads-up — "you're trending over on dining out" — before the month is blown.

Why now: cost-of-living pressure makes a clear, shared budget feel urgent — and AI can finally take the tedium out of categorizing and forecasting, which is what kills the spreadsheet habit.
